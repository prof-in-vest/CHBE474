Model: unsteady 1-D Fick diffusion, ∂C/∂t = D·∂²C/∂x², in a tube whose two ends are held at fixed concentrations C_L and C_R (e.g. two well-mixed reservoirs connected by a stagnant film or narrow capillary). The tube starts uniformly filled with the Fluid 2 concentration, and Fluid 1 diffuses in once contact begins at t = 0. Solved with an explicit finite-difference scheme, stepped live in your browser.
